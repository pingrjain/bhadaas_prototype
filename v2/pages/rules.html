📜 community rules

keeping bhadaas safe, honest, and impactful

✅ what's allowed

  • ✓ honest reviews and experiences with companies, products, and services
  • ✓ tagging companies, organizations, and government bodies
  • ✓ sharing screenshots, images, and evidence to support your experience
  • ✓ constructive criticism, venting, and expressing frustration
  • ✓ emoji reactions to posts you relate to

🚫 what's not allowed

  • ✗ tagging individuals by name — only companies, organizations, and government bodies are allowed. never name or tag a specific person.
  • ✗ hate speech, abuse, or harassment — no racism, sexism, casteism, or discrimination of any kind
  • ✗ false or misleading information — don't make things up. be truthful.
  • ✗ political content — zero tolerance. no political posts, opinions, or party references. ever.
  • ✗ spam or promotional content — no ads, affiliate links (by users), or self-promotion
  • ✗ sharing personal information — no phone numbers, addresses, or personal details of others without consent
  • ✗ fake reviews or competitor sabotage — posting false content to harm a competitor is strictly prohibited

🛡️ moderation process

1

post goes live immediately

your bhadaas is published right away with an "under review" label

2

review by our team

our moderation team reviews the post for guideline compliance

3

approved, edited, or removed

posts that comply are approved. violations may be edited or removed with notification.

🚨 reporting content

if you see content that violates our rules, use the "report" button on any post. you can report for:

🗑️
spam
❌
false or harmful
😡
abuse or harassment
⚖️
legal issue

⚡ consequences

1st violation post removed + warning notification
2nd violation temporary posting suspension (7 days)
3rd violation permanent account suspension
