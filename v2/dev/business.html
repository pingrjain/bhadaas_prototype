₹5k – ₹50k/mo

₹2k – ₹20k/mo

contextual affiliate links in complaint posts — "had a bad experience? try this alternative." commission-based revenue.

low effort

ugc contests with entry fees

₹1k – ₹10k/mo

community contests — best rant of the week, most relatable bhadaas. small entry fees with prize pools.

low effort

featured rant boosting

₹2k – ₹15k/mo

users pay to boost their complaint visibility in the feed. pinned posts, highlighted cards, trending push.

medium effort
5

meme ad drops / collabs

₹10k – ₹75k/mo

brand collaborations for meme-style ad campaigns. viral content partnerships with brands who want the "bhadaas voice".

medium effort
6

bhadaas insider membership

₹3k – ₹30k/mo

premium membership tier — pro badge, priority review, private groups, analytics, trending boost for power users.

medium effort
7

brand response dashboard

₹5k – ₹50k/mo

b2b saas dashboard for companies — respond to complaints, track sentiment, analytics, api access. the real money maker.

highest dev effort
