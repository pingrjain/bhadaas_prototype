this is not a suggestion document — these are non-negotiable rules. violating any of them can kill the company, land founders in court, or cause real-world harm to users.

🏢

register as pvt ltd company

₹8k-₹15k, 2-4 weeks. must be done before any public launch. personal liability protection is non-negotiable for a platform like this.

⚖️

defamation risk is real

budget ₹5-10 lakhs/year legal retainer. you will get sued. it's not "if" — it's "when". have a lawyer on speed dial from day one.

🚫

never allow individual tagging

only companies, organizations, and government bodies. the moment you allow tagging individuals, you become a harassment platform.

🔒

dpdpa 2023 compliance

explicit consent, dpo appointment, data breach notification within 72 hours. fines up to ₹250 crore. this is not optional.

👥

three mandatory officers

grievance officer, nodal contact officer, chief compliance officer. all three required under IT rules 2021. publish names on the site.

🚫

no politics ever

zero tolerance for political content. no political parties, no politicians, no political commentary. this is a consumer complaints platform, not a political forum.

executive summary

what is bhadaas?

bhadaas is india's first dedicated consumer complaints and grievance platform. a bold, unfiltered space where real people share real experiences about companies, organizations, and government bodies. no bots, no fake reviews, no brands buying influence. think of it as "glassdoor for consumers" — transparent, community-driven accountability.

risk assessment

risk matrix

top risks ranked by severity. every item here has a mitigation plan in the relevant sub-page.

1
defamation suits medium

companies will sue. safe harbour under IT act section 79 applies if moderation SOP followed. legal retainer mandatory.

2
individual tagging causing harassment critical

if users can tag individuals, the platform becomes a weapon. this is the #1 existential risk. hard-blocked at architecture level.

3
fake reviews / competitor sabotage high

companies will post fake negative reviews about competitors. ai-powered detection + user reputation scoring mitigates this.

4
political content misuse high

platform could be hijacked for political agendas. zero-tolerance policy, keyword filters, and manual review for flagged content.

5
dpdpa non-compliance high

fines up to ₹250 crore. explicit consent flows, data minimization, dpo appointment, breach notification within 72 hours.

6
IT rules non-compliance medium

loss of safe harbour protection. grievance officer, nodal contact, compliance officer — all three must be appointed and published.

7
content moderation failure at scale medium

as volume grows, manual moderation breaks down. layered approach: ai pre-filter → community reports → human review queue.

8
database breach / PII leak medium

encryption at rest + in transit. pii minimization. no aadhaar/pan storage. regular security audits and penetration testing.

9
user base cold start medium

chicken-and-egg problem. seed with curated content, partner with consumer forums, viral social media campaigns (reels, threads).

10
revenue model validation medium

will companies actually pay for dashboards? validate with 10 pilot companies before scaling. freemium hook essential.
