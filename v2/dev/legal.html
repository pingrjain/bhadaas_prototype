⚖️ legal & compliance

complete legal framework, regulatory compliance, content policies, mandatory officer appointments, and audit checklists for bhadaas.com — operating under indian it law, dpdpa 2023, and intermediary guidelines.

intermediary protection

it act section 79 — safe harbor

the foundation of platform immunity — bhadaas qualifies as an intermediary under the it act, 2000. section 79 provides conditional protection from liability for user-generated content.

🛡️ safe harbor conditions
  • safe harbor protection available for intermediaries — platform is not liable for third-party content if conditions are met
  • condition 1: do not initiate transmission, select receiver, or modify/select content — bhadaas must never edit user posts
  • condition 2: act within 36 hours of receiving a valid complaint or court/government order to remove content
  • condition 3: publish grievance redressal mechanism prominently — users must know how to report issues
  • must comply with it (intermediary guidelines and digital media ethics code) rules, 2021 — updated regulations for social media intermediaries
  • appoint grievance officer — mandatory, name and contact must be displayed on platform
  • appoint nodal contact officer — for 24x7 coordination with law enforcement agencies
  • appoint chief compliance officer — overall compliance oversight, must be resident of india
⚠️ critical: if bhadaas modifies, curates, or editorially controls user content, safe harbor protection under section 79 is LOST. automated moderation (spam filters, profanity filters) is acceptable — editorial changes are not.
data privacy

digital personal data protection act, 2023

india's comprehensive data protection law — applies to all digital personal data processing. bhadaas must comply as a "data fiduciary".

🔐 key requirements
  • obtain explicit, informed consent before collecting any personal data — consent must be specific, clear, and withdrawable
  • appoint data protection officer (dpo) — responsible for data protection compliance and user grievances
  • honor deletion requests within 30 days — users can request complete erasure of their personal data
  • data breach notification within 72 hours — mandatory reporting to the data protection board of india
  • fines up to ₹250 crore for non-compliance — highest penalty category for significant data breaches or willful non-compliance
  • cross-border data transfer restrictions — personal data can only be transferred to countries not blacklisted by the central government
  • children's data requires verifiable parental consent — additional obligations for processing data of minors (under 18)
  • purpose limitation — data collected for one purpose cannot be used for another without fresh consent
🚨 penalty risk: non-compliance with dpdpa can result in penalties up to ₹250 crore per instance. bhadaas must implement consent management, data deletion workflows, and breach notification systems before launch.
legal risk

defamation risk analysis

defamation (civil + criminal under ipc section 499/500) is the primary legal risk for a grievance platform. here's how different scenarios play out.

⚠️ risk scenarios
scenario 1: user posts false claim about a company
→ platform can claim safe harbor IF moderation process exists and content is removed within 36 hours of complaint. company's recourse is against the user, not the platform.
scenario 2: user names an individual person high risk
→ this is why individual tagging is blocked on bhadaas. naming individuals opens both the user AND the platform to defamation suits. individuals have stronger legal protection than companies. platform must proactively prevent this.
scenario 3: company sues platform for defamation
→ need legal retainer on standby. estimated cost: ₹5-10 lakhs/year for a law firm specializing in cyber/it law. first line of defense is safe harbor + immediate content review on complaint.
scenario 4: government complaint about content
→ must comply with government takedown orders immediately. non-compliance can result in loss of safe harbor and criminal proceedings against compliance officer.
💡 defense tips: never modify user content (preserves safe harbor) · act quickly on complaints (36-hour window) · maintain complete audit trail of all moderation actions · log timestamps, ip addresses, and actions taken.
appointments

mandatory officers

under it (intermediary guidelines) rules, 2021 — these 3 officers must be appointed before the platform goes live. all must be residents of india.

📋

grievance officer

primary point of contact for user complaints. name and contact details must be published on the platform.

  • acknowledge complaints within 24 hours
  • resolve complaints within 15 days
  • publish name, email, and phone on platform
  • maintain register of all complaints received
  • provide monthly report to management
🔗

nodal contact officer

liaison with law enforcement agencies and government authorities. must be available 24x7.

  • 24x7 coordination with law enforcement
  • respond to government orders within 72 hours
  • provide information/assistance for investigations
  • coordinate with cert-in for cyber incidents
  • maintain communication log with authorities
🏛️

chief compliance officer

senior employee responsible for overall compliance with it act and intermediary guidelines.

  • overall compliance with it rules 2021
  • ensure platform follows due diligence
  • generate monthly compliance reports
  • coordinate between grievance officer and legal team
  • liable for non-compliance penalties
moderation

content policies

strict content policies designed to minimize legal risk while allowing genuine consumer grievances. the "no individual tagging" rule is the most critical differentiator.

🚫 prohibited content
🔴
no individual tagging (CRITICAL) — only companies, organizations, and government bodies can be tagged. naming individuals is blocked at the ui level and enforced by moderation. this is the single most important rule for defamation defense.
🔴
no political content (zero tolerance) — no posts about political parties, politicians, elections, or political ideology. this avoids sedition charges and government scrutiny.
🟠
no hate speech, abuse, or harassment — posts must focus on the service/product complaint, not personal attacks. profanity filter enforced.
🟠
no personal information sharing — no phone numbers, addresses, aadhaar, pan, or other pii in posts. auto-detected and redacted.
🟡
no fake or misleading content — community reporting + ai detection for fabricated complaints. repeat offenders banned.
🟡
no spam or promotional content — no ads, affiliate links, or promotional posts disguised as complaints.
🚩 report mechanism

every post has a report button with structured categories for faster processing:

🗑️ spam — promotional or irrelevant content
⚠️ false/harmful — fabricated or misleading claims
🔥 abuse — hate speech, harassment, threats
⚖️ legal issue — defamation, pii exposure, court order
data management

data retention policy

balancing law enforcement requirements (it rules mandate data retention) with user privacy rights under dpdpa 2023.

💾 retention rules
  • log ip address, date/time, user-agent for every action — required for law enforcement cooperation under it rules
  • retain user data and logs for minimum 180 days (as per it intermediary guidelines rules, 2021) — even after account deletion
  • user can request deletion of personal data under dpdpa — must be honored within 30 days, but anonymized logs retained for compliance
  • anonymized data retained indefinitely for analytics — stripped of all pii, used for platform insights and trend analysis
  • content moderation logs retained for 1 year — includes all actions taken, timestamps, and officer decisions
  • complaint records and grievance officer responses retained for 2 years — legal requirement for dispute resolution
⚠️ conflict note: there's an inherent tension between it rules (retain 180 days minimum) and dpdpa (delete on request). resolution: anonymize personal data on deletion request but retain non-identifying logs for 180 days. legal counsel should review this approach before implementation.
