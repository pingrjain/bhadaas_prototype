Safety First

Built for Trust, Protected by Law

Bhadaas is designed to protect everyone — the poster, the company, and the platform.

✅
IT Act 2000 Section 79 — Safe Harbor

The platform is legally protected as an intermediary when proper compliance is maintained. Bhadaas follows all Intermediary Guidelines & Digital Media rules and operates entirely within Indian law.

Legally Compliant
🛡️
Your Identity is Protected

Your name never appears publicly. Posts are phone-verified to prevent abuse, but the poster's identity is hidden from all viewers — including companies and their lawyers. Only a valid court order can request user data.

Privacy First
⚖️
Company Right to Respond

Tagged companies can officially respond to any grievance posted. Responses are publicly visible and marked "Official Response". Consumers and companies both get a fair hearing. Transparency is non-negotiable.
