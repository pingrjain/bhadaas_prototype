⚖️ Legal & Compliance Guide

Everything you need to know to legally operate Bhadaas in India. Updated for IT Rules 2021, DPDPA 2023, and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023.

📚 6 Laws Apply ⏱ 36h Compliance Window 💰 ₹250Cr Max Fine 🛡️ Safe Harbor Available
1

Legal Standing — Is Bhadaas Legal?

✅ YES — Bhadaas is legally permissible in India, subject to compliance conditions below.

✅ What Makes It Legal

  • Consumer review platforms are protected as intermediaries under IT Act 2000, Section 79
  • Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015) — intermediary not liable for user content unless they have actual knowledge
  • Consumer Protection Act 2019 actively supports consumer feedback mechanisms
  • MouthShut.com, TripAdvisor India, Google Reviews all operate legally with a similar model
  • Platform does NOT editorialize or endorse individual posts

🚫 What Could Make It Illegal

  • Failing to comply with valid court/government takedown orders within the required window
  • Allowing targeted harassment of private individuals
  • Facilitating doxxing (sharing personal addresses / phone numbers)
  • Allowing political content or election-related content
  • Not removing content after actual knowledge of illegality

IT Act 2000 — Section 79 (Safe Harbor)

Your most important legal protection as an intermediary platform.

🛡️ Your Most Important Legal Protection

Section 79 grants immunity to intermediaries (platforms like Bhadaas) from liability for user-generated content, PROVIDED you meet all conditions below.
Condition What It Means How to Comply Status
Don't initiate content You cannot be the one who posts or modifies content Only users can post — never add editorial commentary to specific posts ✅ Easy
Act on takedown notices Remove content when ordered by courts/government within 36 hours Build a takedown workflow, track all requests, log every compliance action 🟡 Requires Process
Due diligence Follow IT Rules 2021 due diligence requirements Appoint officers, publish policy, quarterly reports 🟡 Requires Setup
Don't select beneficiaries Don't curate content for one entity's benefit Algorithmic ranking only — never manual curation favouring specific companies ✅ Easy

IT Rules 2021 — Intermediary Guidelines

Due diligence requirements that every intermediary must satisfy.

📋 Required from Day 1

✅ Publish Terms of Service (ToS) in English AND at least one Indian language
✅ Publish Privacy Policy (DPDPA-compliant)
✅ Publish Content Policy / Community Guidelines
✅ Appoint Grievance Officer (India-resident, name + contact on website)
✅ Acknowledge grievance within 24 hours, resolve within 15 days

🚀 Required after 50 Lakh Users (SSMI)

📌 Register as Significant Social Media Intermediary (SSMI)
📌 Appoint Resident Grievance Officer (RGO)
📌 Appoint Nodal Contact Officer (NCO) for law enforcement
📌 Appoint Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
📌 Publish monthly compliance report
📌 Provide voluntary user verification mechanism
⚠️ Start compliance from Day 1, even as a small platform. Courts have ruled that non-compliant platforms cannot claim Section 79 protection regardless of size.

DPDPA 2023 — Digital Personal Data Protection Act

🔴 This is the biggest new compliance requirement. DPDPA 2023 is India's GDPR equivalent. Fines go up to ₹250 Crore.

8 Core DPDPA Requirements

These apply to every data principal (user) whose data you process.

1
Informed Consent Must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous
2
Purpose Limitation Collected data used only for the stated, consented purpose
3
Data Minimization Collect only what is strictly necessary for the purpose
4
Accuracy Provide a mechanism for users to correct inaccurate data
5
Storage Limitation Delete personal data when the collection purpose is served
6
Right to Erasure User can request full account deletion; fulfil within 30 days
7
Right to Access User can request a copy of all data you hold about them
8
Breach Notification Notify the Data Protection Board within 72 hours of a data breach

Defamation Law

⚠️ DEFAMATION is your #1 operational risk.

You WILL receive defamation notices. This is not if — it's when. Be prepared with documented procedures before you go live.

Civil vs. Criminal Defamation

Civil Defamation Criminal Defamation
Law BNS 2023, Section 356 (replaces IPC 499/500) Same — BNS 2023, Section 356
Who Can File Any person or company Any person or company
Against Who Platform + Poster Platform + Poster
Platform Liability Intermediary protection applies if compliant Higher risk — government can act suo motu
Penalty (Platform) Damages (monetary) Criminal charges if non-compliant
Mitigation Quick takedown of court-ordered content Same + ensure no editorial endorsement of posts

Key Precedents

Shreya Singhal v. Union of India (2015)
Platforms protected unless actual knowledge + failure to act. Struck down blanket Section 66A.
Google India v. Visaka Industries (2019)
Search/platform not liable for user-generated links and content it merely hosts.
MouthShut.com cases (multiple)
Consumer review platforms have survived multiple defamation suits by maintaining editorial neutrality.
Facebook India v. Union of India
Platform must appoint India-resident officers — non-residency is not a valid defence.

What You Must Do

  • Never add editorial headlines or promotional framing to specific negative posts
  • Always include disclaimer: "Views expressed are solely those of the individual user"
  • Maintain a documented Notice & Takedown procedure, publicly accessible
  • Log every legal notice and your response with timestamps
  • Respond to every notice within 36 hours, even if only to say "under review"

Mandatory Officers — Whom to Appoint

These are legal requirements, not optional roles. Non-compliance voids your Section 79 protection.

🧑‍⚖️

Grievance Officer

Required: IT Rules 2021
Rule Rule 3(2)(a)
Must be India-resident, named on website
Duties Receive complaints, acknowledge 24h, resolve 15 days, monthly reporting
Salary ₹30,000 – ₹80,000 / month
Note Can be same person as CCO initially
👮

Nodal Contact Officer

Required at 50L+ Users
Rule IT Rules 2021, Rule 3(2)(b) — SSMI only
Must be India-resident senior employee
Duties Liaison for law enforcement; respond to police/court within 72h
Salary Senior employee level
Note Can be the Grievance Officer initially
🔐

Data Protection Officer

Required: DPDPA 2023
Act DPDPA 2023
Must be Qualified in data protection law
Duties Oversee data processing, handle DPDPA requests, interface with Data Protection Board
Salary ₹60,000 – ₹1.5L / month
Note Can hire externally — vCDPO service available

Content Policy Requirements

What your publicly published Content Policy must cover.

🚫 Prohibited Content

✅ Doxxing — personal addresses or phone numbers of private individuals
✅ Threats of violence or harm
✅ Sexual content of any kind
✅ Political content and election-related posts
✅ Content about minors
✅ Sharing personal financial details of others (bank account numbers, card details)

⚠️ Recommended Restrictions

⚠️ Individual person tagging restricted to public figures (CEOs, politicians — professional capacity only)
⚠️ Government officer complaints restricted to service quality — not personal attacks
⚠️ Cap at 3 posts per user per day to prevent coordinated spam campaigns

📋 Process Requirements

✅ Describe clearly how users can report content
✅ Describe appeals process for rejected posts
✅ Describe how you handle legal notices and court orders

State-Specific Risks

India is not one jurisdiction — individual states add complexity.

ℹ️ Individual states have additional laws and courts that may affect operations. Moderation SLAs and content scrutiny should be calibrated by region.
State Risk Level Specific Law / Issue Mitigation
All India 🟡 Standard IT Act + BNS — Defamation, Section 79 Standard compliance procedures
Uttar Pradesh 🔴 High Higher political sensitivity; aggressive local defamation cases Extra moderation for UP-tagged entities
Maharashtra 🟡 Medium High corporate density; Mumbai companies have large legal departments Faster response SLA for Mumbai-based cases
Delhi 🔴 High Government offices + courts; high risk of government body complaints Dedicated moderation for Delhi government entities
Tamil Nadu 🟡 Medium Language — Tamil medium complaints; DPDPA includes regional language rights Support Tamil language posts in v2
West Bengal 🔴 High Political sensitivity; high political organisation activity Strict no-political-content enforcement; extra flags for WB
