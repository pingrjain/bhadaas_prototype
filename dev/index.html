STOP. Read This Before You Build.

This page outlines critical legal, financial, and operational risks you must understand before developing, hosting, or operating Bhadaas in India.

Last Updated: May 2026
🏛️

LEGAL ENTITY REQUIRED

You CANNOT operate this as a freelancer or individual. Must register as Private Limited Company (or LLP). Reason: IT Rules 2021 require a registered entity with appointed officers. Timeline: 2–4 weeks. Cost: ₹8,000–₹15,000.

⚖️

DEFAMATION RISK IS REAL

Companies WILL send legal notices. Budget ₹5–10 lakhs/year for legal retainer from Day 1. Some notices will demand post removal. You have 36 hours to comply with valid court orders. Not complying = you lose safe harbor immunity under IT Act Section 79.

🚫

NEVER ALLOW INDIVIDUAL TAGGING

Tagging private individuals (not public figures) is the SINGLE highest risk action. Can result in harassment, stalking facilitation, criminal defamation charges. Version 1 must restrict to Companies, Orgs, and Government Bodies ONLY.

🔐

DATA PRIVACY = NON-NEGOTIABLE

DPDPA 2023 (India's new data law) is now in force. You MUST: Get explicit consent, appoint a DPO (Data Protection Officer), honor deletion requests within 30 days, store data only in India (data localization may apply). Fines: up to ₹250 Crore.

🏛️

THREE MANDATORY OFFICERS

IT Rules 2021 require you to publicly name and provide contact for: (1) Grievance Officer — must be India-resident, respond within 15 days; (2) Nodal Contact Officer — for law enforcement requests; (3) Chief Compliance Officer — for legal compliance. These must be on your website.

🚫

NO POLITICS. EVER.

Tagging politicians, political parties, or government officials personally is a fast track to: Section 66A revival risk, defamation by powerful entities, shutdown orders. Focus ONLY on consumer services, not political opinions. Have a ZERO tolerance content policy for political content.

Platform Overview

Bhadaas — What It Is

Bhadaas (भड़ास) is a verified consumer grievance & experience-sharing platform exclusively for India. Unlike buried forums, Bhadaas is loud, visible, and action-oriented. Users post verified complaints tagging companies. Companies pay to respond and access data. Everyone wins — except bad actors.

The platform fills a massive gap: India's 1.4 billion consumers have almost no effective, public-facing tool to escalate and share service failures. Bhadaas makes accountability visible and searchable.

Founding Sequence

Before You Write a Single Line of Code

This is the correct sequence of actions for founding Bhadaas

1
🏢

Register the Company

Bhadaas Technologies Private Limited. Get a CA, takes 2–4 weeks, costs ₹8,000–₹15,000. Get Pvt Ltd, not proprietorship. This is the legal foundation everything else depends on.

2
⚖️

Hire Legal Counsel

Engage a tech/media law firm (Nishith Desai Associates, Khaitan & Co, or similar). Budget ₹3–5 lakhs for the first year for retainer. Do not compromise on this — legal exposure is your #1 risk.

3
📋

Draft Legal Documents

Terms of Service, Privacy Policy (DPDPA-compliant), Content Policy. Do NOT copy from another site. Must be custom drafted by your lawyer. These documents are your legal shield.

4
👤

Appoint Mandatory Officers

Grievance Officer (India-resident, must respond within 15 days), Nodal Contact Officer (for law enforcement), DPO (Data Protection Officer). These can be the same person initially — just name them publicly.

5
🛡️

Get D&O Insurance

Directors & Officers liability insurance. Covers personal liability if the company is sued. This protects you personally — if a company sues and wins judgment against the company, D&O covers the directors. Required from Day 1 of operations.

6
🔐

Setup DPDPA Compliance Infrastructure

Consent management system, data retention policy, deletion workflow (honor within 30 days), data localization plan. This is non-negotiable — fines can reach ₹250 Crore for serious violations.

7
💻

Then Start Building

MVP scope: phone verification, post creation, entity tagging (companies only), Me Too upvotes, basic AI moderation. Absolutely no individual tagging in v1. Keep it focused and shippable.

8
🚀

Pilot in One City First

Delhi or Mumbai. 2–3 categories only — Telecom, Banking, E-commerce. Control the content volume so moderation is manageable. Gather data, learn fast, refine before scaling nationally.
