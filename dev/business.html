Revenue Model Overview

Why This Business Makes Money

💡 Key Insight: Bhadaas makes money from companies (B2B), NOT from users. Users are always free. This ensures maximum user growth while companies pay for access to that audience.
PRIMARY · Est. 60% of revenue
🏢

Company Subscriptions

Companies pay monthly for visibility, analytics, and response capability.

  • Basic ₹9,999/month
  • Professional ₹29,999/month
  • Enterprise ₹99,999+/month
  • Trigger: Company has >20 Bhadaas posts
TRANSACTIONAL · Est. 15% of revenue
💬

Pay-Per-Response

Basic plan companies pay ₹1,999 per official response to a Bhadaas post.

  • ₹1,999 per official response (Basic plan)
  • Delivered to OP + ALL Me Too users
  • Higher plans include unlimited responses
  • Psychology: silence = guilt perception
PREMIUM · Est. 10% of revenue
📊

Data & Analytics Reports

Detailed intelligence reports for companies, analysts, media, and government.

  • One-time entity report: ₹4,999
  • Crisis alert package: ₹14,999/month
  • API access: ₹50K–₹5L/year
  • Buyers: research firms, analysts, media, govt
INNOVATIVE · Est. 8% of revenue
✅

Resolution Marketplace

Companies pay per verified resolution — incentivising actual problem-solving.

  • ₹499–₹2,999 per verified resolution
  • Resolution badge visible publicly
  • User confirms resolution (prevents gaming)
  • Reputation benefit drives adoption
SUPPLEMENTAL · Est. 5% of revenue
👑

Premium User Features

Optional Bhadaas Pro for power users — keeps 95% of features free.

  • Bhadaas Pro: ₹199/month
  • Priority posting, direct escalation
  • Detailed post tracking & analytics
  • Upsell: "Upgrade to priority position"
SCALABLE · Est. 12% of revenue
🗃️

Aggregated Data Licensing

Anonymized consumer sentiment data as a sellable economic indicator.

  • Pricing: ₹50K–₹5L/year per license
  • Buyers: investment firms, market research
  • RBI data teams, media & think-tanks
  • Consumer sentiment as economic signal

Admin Panel Features

Complete admin capabilities required to operate the platform at scale.

🛡️ Content Moderation

  • AI-powered auto-moderation (keyword detection, spam, doxxing prevention)
  • Human review queue for flagged content
  • Batch approve/reject with reason logging
  • Edit functionality — remove PII, not content substance
  • Appeals management with 7-day window

🏢 Entity Management

  • Entity creation, editing, and categorisation
  • Duplicate detection and intelligent merging
  • Company claim verification (document review workflow)
  • Category management and taxonomy control

👤 User Management

  • Verification status management (Bronze → Platinum)
  • Trust score manual adjustment with audit log
  • Suspension and permanent ban workflow
  • Identity verification response (for legal requests only)
  • Users remain anonymous publicly at all times

💳 Revenue Management

  • Subscription management (upgrades, downgrades, cancellations)
  • Automated invoice generation
  • Churn tracking and early-warning dashboard
  • Usage-based billing for pay-per-response

⚖️ Legal Compliance

  • Legal notice logging with timestamp and source
  • Takedown workflow with mandatory 36h compliance timer
  • Court order tracking and response log
  • DPDPA deletion requests queue
  • Quarterly transparency report generation
  • Grievance Appellate Committee liaison workflow

Growth Strategy

A phased, city-first launch reduces risk and validates product-market fit before scaling.

Phased Launch Strategy

0–6 Months
Phase 1

Seed & Validate

  • Scope Delhi or Mumbai only
  • Categories Telecom, Banking, E-commerce only
  • Platform Web only (mobile-responsive)
  • Moderation Manual team of 2
  • Seeding Founders + early users post 200 Bhadaas manually
  • Goal 5,000 users, 1,000 posts, 5 company sign-ups
Revenue target: ₹1–2L/month
6–18 Months
Phase 2

Scale

  • Scope Pan-India (top 10 cities)
  • Categories All 12 categories
  • Platform Web + React Native mobile app
  • Moderation AI-assisted + 5 human moderators
  • Marketing PR stories, resolved complaint case studies, social media
  • Goal 5,00,000 users, 50,000 posts, 200 company sign-ups
Revenue target: ₹50–80L/month
18–36 Months
Phase 3

Monetize & Expand

  • Scope All India
  • Platform Web + Mobile + API
  • Partnerships RBI, TRAI, Consumer Forums data exchange
  • Enterprise Top 50 companies on platform
  • Funding Series A ₹15–25 Cr at ₹100 Cr valuation
  • Exit options CRISIL, ICRA, Reliance, Tata acquisition
Revenue target: ₹3–5 Crore/month
